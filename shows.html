Sight & Sound Theatres

Massive wrap-around stage productions bringing epic stories to life with live animals, soaring sets, and original orchestral scores.

THEME PARK Indian Point Rd

Silver Dollar City

An 1880s-style Ozark theme park packed with record-breaking coasters, live bluegrass craftsmen, and famous seasonal festivals.

Visitor Tip: Arrive 45 minutes before rope drop and use the back-road route via Shepherd of the Hills Expressway to skip Hwy 76 traffic.
Compare Park Tickets →
FAMILY VARIETY W 76 Country Blvd

The Haygoods

High-energy sibling show featuring over 20 instruments, modern country and rock hits, tap dancing, and special light effects.

Visitor Tip: Great choice for multi-generational groups with kids and teens.
View Ticket Options →
COMEDY & COUNTRY W 76 Country Blvd

Presleys' Country Jubilee

Branson's original Strip theater family blending classic country, southern gospel, and slapstick Ozark comedy.

Visitor Tip: Try the intermission treats in the lobby—a longtime Branson tradition.

DINNER CRUISE Table Rock Lake

Showboat Branson Belle

A two-hour paddlewheel cruise across scenic Table Rock Lake complete with a three-course meal and live variety entertainment.

Visitor Tip: Sunset cruises offer the best lake views from the top wrap-around decks.
